[Intro]
In the heart of silence, where echoes once dwelled
A musical relay begins its tale
Four voices gather, like runners at the start
Each with a song, about to play their part

[Verse 1]
In the silence, I begin
A melody that echoes within
My voice rises high and clear
As I pass the line to one who's here

[Chorus]
Chain of sound, never broken
No two sing at once, no room for disgrace
A chain of sound, no louder or loud
Just one voice at a time, forever profound

[Verse 2]
I hand it over, smooth and slow
My voice disappears as it goes
Crossfades gentle, no pause or break
This is the relay, for all to partake

[Chorus]
Chain of sound, never broken
No two sing at once, no room for disgrace
A chain of sound, no louder or loud
Just one voice at a time, forever profound

[Verse 3]
Notes float between invisible lines
Like fireflies in the moonlit skies
One fades as another takes its place
A symphony of whispers, not a trace

[Bridge]
The round amplified; the relay transmits
The crowd dissolves, the ledger stays
Every handoff a small ceremony
Every fade a structure, every breath a phase

[Outro]
And so the relay ends where it began
Four voices' song, in rhythm and in span
One voice, finishing, alone at last
With no one left to hand the line to
The song is not louder — it is continuous
