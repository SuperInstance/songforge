(Verse 1)
Four voices blend in harmony's song,
In perfect rounds, they start their throng.
First comes A, then B, swift and clear,
C lingers, echoing the ear's cheer.

(Chorus)
Round and round we sing our part,
Four voices strong, no need for dart.
Yet C drifts slower, time to pace,
In this musical canon, it finds its space.

(Verse 2)
A leans in, B's rhythm takes the lead,
Now it's B's turn to hold the reed.
C, still behind, whispers deep,
The song becomes C's solo keep.

(Chorus)
Round and round they sing our tune,
Four voices dancing under moon.
But C lags, its pace does not falter,
In this musical circle, it matters.

(Verse 3)
A steps back, B moves into view,
Now C's melody rings anew.
B's speed increases, then passes by,
Leaving A and C in a soft sigh.

(Chorus)
Round and round they've sung their song,
Four voices once, now just two, where they belong.
C drifts, its echo soft and clear,
In this musical dance, it holds dear.

(Verse 4)
A pauses, B fades out, C's final note,
Echoes linger, yet we've got.
The slowest voice, now solo strong,
In rewind, silence sings its song.

(Chorus (Reversed))
From C's depth, the silence begins,
As it flows like water in a kenning.
A and B listen, hearts alight,
Now they're the voices in the night.

(Outro)
C whispers last, its tale told,
In this musical round, a memory enfolds.
When reversed, silence takes the stage,
In this canon's cycle, it shall engage.
