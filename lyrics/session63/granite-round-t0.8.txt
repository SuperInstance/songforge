(Verse 1)
Four voices join in harmony, a musical dance,
In unison they sing, a delicate trance.
First to enter, clear and bright, like morning's glow,
Their notes weave strong, in rhythmic flow.

(Chorus)
We're four voices round, in perfect song,
Each one takes its turn, as the music's throng.
Yet, one lingers, slower, deeper tone,
A subtle echo, our musical stone.

(Verse 2)
The second voice arrives, lighter, more a haze,
Its notes blend with first, in a gentle blaze.
Then third and fourth enter, sharp and bright,
Their rhythm steady, guiding the sight.

(Chorus)
We're four voices round, in harmony's hold,
Each one takes its part, like stories untold.
One remains, a whisper in the mix,
A subtle echo, our musical fix.

(Verse 3)
The slow voice now leads, deep and rich,
Its notes swell, as if from life's pit.
While others fade, their energy spent,
This lone voice, unyielding, never bent.

(Chorus)
We're four voices round, in this dance so grand,
One by one, they take their stand.
But when spun backward, silence reigns,
To the end where our slow voice remains.

(Verse 4)
As the music turns, quiet becomes loud,
Silence moves to the other side of the crowd.
The last note echoes, soft and clear,
A testament to the round we've heard.

(Chorus)
We're four voices in a musical spiral,
One by one, we share our whispered thrill.
When played backward, silence does reside,
In this round, our slow voice is our guide.
