VERSE 1:
In whispers so soft, they blend,
Time twists like a fleeting scene.
Voices intertwine in symphony,
Echoes of harmony begin.

CHORUS:
Backwards, the song starts at last,
Ending silence, begins anew.
Whispers blend, voices join,
Round by round, forever entwined.
