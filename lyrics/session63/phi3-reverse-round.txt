
(Verse)  
In echoes of time we dance backwards twine,   
Four hands entwined in canonic binds,   
Till one voice falls silent and then I hear the lasting plea - "No more," whispered with a sigh.    

(Chorus)  
We start as whispers on an ending'th chord, so dire; our voices now reversed in time’s grand tour:   
The first voice echoes back through the night to find its place and power once again within this backwards lore.  

(Verse 2)    
A trio finds solace as they sing just a while more, their melody like droplets on glass - distinct yet part of one greater song:   
Then silence befalls us all but for the singular echo that resonates alone and clear throughout this inverted realm.  

(Chorus)    
With every note our journey turns complete; we begin as whispers in reverse, singing back through time’s door - a quartet of voices now reduced to one:   
This lone whisper holds the essence pure with its simple truth and stands solitary upon this stage where once four had reigned.  

(Verse 3)    
A voice fades as another rises, their harmonies entangled in time’s fabric - a single thread among many that bind:   
Then silence is the one to greet with open arms until our melodic journey finds its start anew within this reverse-spun tale.  

(Chorus)    
As we ascend back through notes undone, each voice returns in time’s twisted flow - a quartet of souls once more:   
In the end is but one voice left standing alone to carry forth our story's echoes and lead us from this realm where silence reigns.  

(Verse 4)    
The first, with trembling fingers strumming strings that mimic heartbeats of an unfamiliar rhythm - a touch upon the keys like falling leaves:   
Then as one voice fades into nothing but air and memory's faint whisper becomes our guide through this backwards lullaby.  

(Chorus)    
We begin where we end, in silence now grown; yet from stillness breaks forth life anew - for even reversed melodies hold the power to move us:   
And as one voice rises once more with a gentle sigh upon this stage of quiet that time's mirror has wrought, our song begins again.
