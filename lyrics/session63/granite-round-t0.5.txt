(Verse 1)
Four voices start in harmony, each with a beat,
In a round, they sing, their melodies complete.
First enters, swift and clear, the leader's call,
Then follows, steady, second, strong and tall.
Third arrives, soft yet bright, like morning's gentle ray,
Last comes fourth, deep and resonant, in awe we sway.

(Chorus)
We dance in rhythm, this musical sphere,
Four voices strong, together they're clear.
But one lingers, slow and steady,
In this round, it's the key to unity.

(Verse 2)
Second now, a moment slower, keeps the pace,
While third and fourth maintain their space.
The leader's call grows faint, then fades away,
Leaving only one, in its quiet ballet.

(Chorus)
We danced in unison, this symphony,
But now, one voice remains, steady and free.
When played backward, silence takes the stage,
And our round, once whole, now finds its age.

(Bridge)
As time reverses, so does the pause,
In this reversed round, we find cause.
Silence moves to where it began,
A testament to our musical plan.

(Verse 3)
The leader's call, now silent, whispers, "I,"
Echoing through the space, its truth to apply.
Second, third, and fourth in harmony,
Now sing with one, as if they're just one more.

(Chorus)
We've reversed our round, this tale of two,
One voice remains, a constant view.
When played backward, silence finds its place,
And our musical dance, once in disarray, embraces grace.

(Outro)
In every note, in every pause,
Lives the memory of this round's cause.
Four voices, one, now two, then four,
This is the story of our musical lore.
