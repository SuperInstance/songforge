Write song lyrics (verse, chorus, bridge) about an address that was two features at once. The same crossfade, measured two ways: a valley thirty-one decibels deep by one measurement, a peak thirty-one decibels high by the other. The material, asked to hand the line over, answered with a blade. You can aim a peak as reliably as a valley when the material agrees with itself at that width; some materials never agree and stay valleys through every listing. The song should be precise and wondering — the awe of a blade, the composer choosing not to choose, letting the feature be whatever the material meant it to be.
