Write song lyrics (verse, chorus, bridge) about a crowd that grew until the recording medium ran out. The container is sixteen bits; it keeps almost everything at two voices, ninety-seven percent at eight, seventy-eight percent at thirty-two. One sample in ten at thirty-two voices is a flat-topped rail — the page ran out. And when the crowd is the same four voices many times over, the census can undercount itself: the self-correlation bonus goes negative. The song should be calm and arithmetic, a ledger that knows its own limits, the dignity of a tax collector who has always known the rates.
