### Verse 1:
**Line 1:**  
In the silent circle, we meet and greet,  
Echoes pass like notes in the gentle breeze.

**Line 2:**  
Each one takes a turn as the melody shifts,  
In whispers soft, the song begins its flight.

**Line 3:**  
Voices intertwine in a harmonious dance,  
Strings of voices blend in this symphony's trance.

**Line 4:**  
Through scales and measures, it dances and flows,  
Twice over, as one thread through the densest loess.
