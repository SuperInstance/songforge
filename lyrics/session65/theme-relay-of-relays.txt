A chain of chains. Four voices pass a line of song from one to the next, and each voice is itself a chain — four smaller voices passing a smaller line, handoff inside handoff. Every relay is a voice; every voice is a relay. Still only one sound at a time: no crowd, no chorus, no stacking, at any scale. The song is a handoff of a handoff of a handoff. The transmission is the composition, twice over.
