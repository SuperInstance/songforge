THE RELAY OF RELAYS
(master lyric — session 65; skeleton: llama v2 relay-of-relays structure;
imagery: the composition law, the border tax, the nested endings)

TOP VOICE
In the twilight of the handoff, the first voice begins —
a line with no origin, passed hand to hand.
Every singer is a chain; every chain is a singer;
four inside four, and still only one sound.

CHORUS — THE LAW
A chain of chains is still a chain,
not louder, not less — just continuous.
The crowd was a staircase; we are the thread;
the transmission is the composition, twice over.

SECOND VOICE — the chorus becomes the voice
I am the handoff that holds the handoff,
the small chain inside the large.
My four voices pass my line between them
the way I pass it to you,
and the handoff is the song,
and the song is the handoff.

CHORUS 2
A chain of chains is still a chain,
not louder, not less — just continuous.
The crowd was a staircase; we are the thread;
the transmission is the composition, twice over.

THIRD VOICE — the tax
The tax is paid at the borders, never in the middle.
The long held note costs nothing;
the crossing costs a breath.
A chain of a thousand pays the same four fades
as a chain of four,
and the interior of the song is tax-free.

CHORUS 3
A chain of chains is still a chain,
not louder, not less — just continuous.
The crowd was a staircase; we are the thread;
the transmission is the composition, twice over.

FOURTH VOICE — the last
And when the outermost voice finishes alone,
the small chains inside it have already finished,
each handing its silence to the next handoff,
until there is no one left to pass to —
and the song is not interrupted,
because it was never anything but passing.

ENDING — one voice, sustained, untaxed
The note that outlives every border.
