Title: Theme Relay of Relays

(Verse 1)
In the heart of the city, under neon's glow,
Lies a track, a battleground for runners' show.
The night is long, but the spirit never fades,
A relay race, where stories are made.

(Chorus)
Theme Relay, a symphony of feet,
Carrying tales on every beat.
Each runner, a messenger, with a secret to share,
In this dance of time, they wear the flare.

(Verse 2)
First comes the first leg, swift and light,
A whisper of secrets, in the quiet night.
Second, a burst, passionate and bold,
Unveiling tales of love, of dreams untold.

(Chorus)
Theme Relay, a harmony of voices,
Echoing through the city's silent noises.
Third leg, strong and steady, like an oak,
Telling stories of resilience, of battles fought.

(Bridge)
Fourth, a poet, with words as their weapon,
Painting vivid scenes, in a rhythm so sweet.
Fifth, a musician, strumming life's chords,
Stirring emotions, from the heart's core.

(Chorus)
Theme Relay, a melody intertwined,
A testament to humanity, in every line.
Sixth leg, graceful, like a dancer's sway,
Bringing tales of beauty, in the soft moonlight's ray.

(Outro)
As the final runner crosses the finish line,
The theme relay ends, but stories remain, enshrined.
In every heartbeat, in every shared breath,
The Theme Relay lives on, a testament to wealth.
