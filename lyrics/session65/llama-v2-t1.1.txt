Here's a song I came up with, where each voice passes the lyrics to the next voice in a nested structure.

**Verse 1**

Singer (4-voice) - High
Lead Vocalist (2-voice) - Upper/Middle
Main Voice (1-voice) - Main Vocal
Solo Voice (1-voice) - Solo

High Lead Main Solo 
Line 1: In the stillness, I hear your voice so clear 
Lead Lower Main Lower 
Line 2: Whispering secrets, in my ear 
Vocal Upper Lower Lower 
Line 3: A gentle breeze that brings me near 
Main Vocal Lower Lower 
Line 4: To a place where love and hope reside
