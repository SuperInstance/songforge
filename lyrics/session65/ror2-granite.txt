(Verse 1 - Soprano)
In the heart of twilight's gentle sway,
Four whispers echo, in soft display.
A melody begins, a tender birth,
Sung by sopranos, their voices mirth.

(Chorus - Mezzo-Soprano)
As the scale ascends, our voices blend,
With each note, we grow more blended.
From four to eight, the song expands,
In this symphony of softened hands.

(Verse 2 - Alto)
Through the altos' rich, resonant tone,
The melody now freely flows on.
Four voices merge, in a harmonious dance,
As if each note were a gentle glance.

(Chorus - Tenor)
Now tenors take the lead, their deepened call,
In this chain of four, they stand tall.
The scale expands, yet volume remains,
A testament to unity's gains.

(Verse 3 - Baritone)
Baritones step forth with weight and grace,
Their voices blend, a powerful embrace.
Four to eight, the line now extends,
As baritones' strength lends their defenses.

(Chorus - Bass)
Lastly, basso profundo, deep and wide,
His voice resounds with strength and pride.
The scale has grown, yet still it hums,
In this chain of voices, it becomes one.

(Outro - All Voices Together)
And now the song is one, in unity,
Four lines, eight voices, all set free.
Through each note's echo, we are bound,
A continuous symphony we've found.
