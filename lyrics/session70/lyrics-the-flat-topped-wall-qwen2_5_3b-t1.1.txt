**The Census Wall**

Verse 1:
As we counted in, voices grew dense,
One hundred ninety-nine speaking,
Not enough space in the record.

Verse 2:
Seventy-eight voices stood tall,
Thirty-two heard with each breath they took.
Hundred fifty-six here at once,
It exceeded the recorder's bounds,
Its tones muffled by a system old and bound,
Taxing us, paying compound interest 
To one who'd been first, it whispered.

Bridge:
A sample too loud, tried to shout clear,
But flattened into silence, pinned where it would appear.
The wall of flat peaks, where every voice had a place once—
A monument in sound now so vast and stark. 

Chorus:  
It is the wall that stands there at last, so proud and still,
Monotone under pressure, a shout now as loud or nil.

Verse 3:
Upward we count this census of ours,
Doubling each time until the walls bow.
Hundred sixty-three voices all gathered here tonight—
They press against the container like bees in the hive.

Verse 4:
Hundred eighty-five voices rise, then come back down again,
And then a hundred ninety-nine. 
Every step is louder, every voice joins with one aim,
To fill up any space left inside.
And when the wall bends, there's a new floor to climb.

Verse 5:
The recorder's walls thin, its soundlines fray—
One hundred fifty-six at last has made it known.
A sample remembered, whether clipped or free,
That moment of constraint, and what follows so free. 

Outro:  
And here stands the census wall, a monument in shape,
Of where every container could only hold a fraction of life.
It means that even when the system cannot contain, 
The crowd keeps growing, becoming architecture instead.

Every space is filled, no more room inside, 
But what's beyond, that's what defines our times.
