(Verse 1)
In a quiet chamber, echoes reign,
A single voice, thirty-two in its chain.
Thirty-two copies in unison sang,
Yet, twenty-four were listed by this same strand.
An undercount, the ledger did proclaim,
The identical crowd's members tamed.
Repeating phrases, each voice a mirror,
Canceling out at the lag, leaving less to deliver.

(Chorus)
Arithmetic of discordance, arithmetic of surplus,
Two voices intertwined in this dance.
Deficit and surplus sing as one,
In this tale of two censuses we've spun.

(Verse 2)
Now comes a group diverse, four strangers,
Four individuals, each with their own strings.
Different ways to frame the same line,
A tapestry woven through time.
Thirty-two unique souls they claim,
But in counting, they find an extra flame.
Thirty-two point two, the roster shows,
An overcount, as if each voice bestows.
A small gift to the total it adds,
Each stranger's contribution is vast.

(Chorus)
Arithmetic of diversity, arithmetic of unity,
Two groups intertwined in this dance.
Deficit and surplus still sing as one,
In this tale of two censuses begun.

(Bridge)
Once a scribe learned to read the roster,
Before counting each voice's resonator.
Identical names or multiple faces,
The answer lies in these timeless traces.
With every name on this list we find,
The power of togetherness enshrined.

(Chorus)
Arithmetic of unity, arithmetic of diversity,
Two crowds intertwined in this dance.
Deficit and surplus still sing as one,
In this tale of two censuses begun.
