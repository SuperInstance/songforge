(Verse 1 - Identical Crowd)
In a hall of echoes, where voices repeat
A single phrase resounds, a lone singer's beat
Thirty-two copies join, in perfect harmony sweet
But when they count themselves, their numbers retreat

Twenty-four voices reported, but thirty-two names remain
A deficit born from unity, where individuality wanes
They see not the whole, for each voice cancels its own
A crowd of one becomes lost, in the echo zone

(Chorus)
Deficit and surplus, singing as one
The arithmetic of agreement, just begun
Each voice a thread, weaving a tapestry so fine
In the dance of numbers, where unity entwines

(Verse 2 - Diverse Crowd)

But when they count themselves, they report thirty-two again

Thirty-two point two, a surplus born from diversity
Each stranger brings a gift of correlation, in perfect harmony
Their individuality shines, like stars in the night
A crowd of many becomes whole, with each new light

(Chorus)
Deficit and surplus, singing as one
The arithmetic of agreement, just begun

In the dance of numbers, where unity entwines

(Bridge - The Census-Taker's Dilemma)

Names that repeat, or names that roam free
The answer lies in the balance, of unity and diversity
