(Verse 1)
In a crowd of one, a solitary sound
Thirty-two voices, all in harmony bound
But when they counted themselves, it was a sorry sight
Twenty-four souls, in a ledger's dim light
The lag of agreement, a gap so wide
A deficit of truth, where numbers did reside

(Chorus)
Add and subtract, the arithmetic sway
Deficit and surplus, sing their song each day
In the census room, the numbers take stage
But only when we read, do we turn the page

(Verse 2)
Then came a crowd of strangers, diverse and free
Four singers, four lives, with a single plea
They sang in harmony, but counted as one
Thirty-two point two, their surplus just begun
Their voices blended, like a work of art
A collective echo, that touched the heart

(Bridge)
The census-taker learned, to read the roster with care
To see if names were many times, or just one there
A single name repeated, like a refrain in the air
Or many names once, like threads of a tapestry so rare

(Verse 3)
Now we see ourselves, in this second crowd's gaze
The many voices, their individual ways
Their diverse perspectives, weave a rich and complex sound
And in our own counting, we find our truth profound
