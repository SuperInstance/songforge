**Verse 1:**
In a room where we all tried to speak,
The tax was monotone, a steady peak.
Ninety-nine percent held their peace,
Ninety-seven at eight, seventy-eight at thirty-two.

**Chorus:**
And then the census grew past its limit,
One hundred fifty-six voices in the room,
The recording could no longer hold them all,
A wall of perfect flat tops, a single shout.

**Bridge:**
Remembering what it sounded like before,
If I can be clipped or free from this roar?
In the moment when the medium said no,
What is the shape of architecture now?
