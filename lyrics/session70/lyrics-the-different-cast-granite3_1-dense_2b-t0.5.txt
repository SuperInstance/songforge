Title: "Censused Echoes"

(Verse 1 - Identical Crowd)
In a chamber, one voice echoed thirty-two times,
A single singer's song, each note precise.
Thirty-two copies agreed in unison,
Yet the ledger whispered twenty-four, a deficit,
The identical crowd undercounting itself,
Mirroring the mystery of one's own presence.

(Chorus - Arithmetic)
Twenty-four plus thirty-two equals fifty-six,
A sum that hides the truth, a number it did not sign.
This is where the identical crowd stood,
Counting themselves short by two, their numbers unsound.

(Verse 2 - Diverse Crowd)
Now enter four strangers, each with their own tale,
Four lives intertwined, yet separate in scale.
Four voices distinct, yet singing the same line,
Thirty-two names reported as thirty-two point two,
An overcount, a surplus, a testament to unity,
Each new voice adding a tiny bit of correlation.

(Chorus - Arithmetic)
Thirty-two plus three equals thirty-five,
A sum that reveals the truth, a number it did not hide.
Here lies the diverse crowd's answer, their numbers complete.

(Bridge - The Census-Taker's Learning)
The census-taker learned to read the roster first,
To see the names as one or many, never half-past.
For in this tale of two crowds, that is the key,
The truth lies in understanding how each voice will be.

(Chorus - Arithmetic)
Fifty-six plus thirty-five equals ninety-one,
A sum that sings the story, a number it did not shun.
This is where the diverse crowd found their rest,
Counting themselves accurately, their numbers blessed.

(Outro - Unity)
And so, two censuses told of the same voice,
One repeated, many strangers, each a choice.
The identical crowd and the diverse one,
Both counted right, yet different in their own way,
Revealing that unity can be found in both abundance and scarcity.
