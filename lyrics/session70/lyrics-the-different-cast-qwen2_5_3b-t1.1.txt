### Verse 1  
In a chorus so vast and wide,  
One voice echoes back in time.  
Repeats its refrain with thirty-two peers,  
Agrees but misses by a sheen.

The tally shows twenty-four voices,  
Where thirty-two names were listed for sure.
A deficit of two to its count,
A gap that masks the crowd from sight.

### Bridge
Before they number and call,
The census-taker learns each name's tale. 
In rooms full of echoes, one is all,  
Names might repeat but not in vain.

### Verse 2  
Four strangers add to the tally too,  
Voices different yet one line does share.
Three twenty-ones become thirty-two point two,  
Each new stranger brings a bit more alignment.

Two crowds apart, counting alike first,  
Then distinct, four echoes merge and split,
Repeating phrases now with a dozen voices,  
The chorus of names begins to clarify.

### Chorus
Deficit sings low like the crowd's despair,  
Surplus hums high, as one in two is rare.
Each echo whispers its part, each name’s claim,  
Tallying up truths where no single song can stay.

### Verse 3  
In numbers so diverse,  
A crowd of one echoes more confused.
Where thirty-two names are known as twenty-four voices at best.  
They cancel out in a way that's not quite right.

But add to the tally strangers who come and go,  
Voices different yet each adding to the fold,
Thirty-two point two now stands tall,
Each new addition with its own small connection bold.

### Bridge
The census-taker watches their roster keenly,  
Names repeat or names are a new set.
Two counts in one room: which is it?  
One voice many times or many voices anew?

### Chorus  
Where one voice tries to be true and large,  
Four strangers make clear the tally's range.  
Deficit whispers low, surplus swells high,  
In arithmetic truths align.

### Bridge
The names may loop in echoes vast and wide,  
But every new name brings a little light.
Whether many echo once or one repeats many times over,
That’s the answer we seek with the final verse.

### Verse 4  
Here's the lesson from numbers they see,  
Counting truths without losing the feel. 
One voice may hide in its repetitions vast and wide,  
While multiple voices add new connections light.

The truth is not in numbers alone but the space between,  
Where each number adds to the whole, whether true or just a shade.

### Chorus
In this song of census and rhyme,  
We see two counts where they line up fine.  
Whether one or many, each speaks its part,  
A symphony where truths come not in ranks but in heart.

The bridge learns to read the room with eyes new,
Names repeat or names are unique, 
But together, like our chorus, we make truth known,  
Wherever they count and how many.
