**Verse 1 - Tight Width**
In a realm where voices intertwine,
A meter read the seam with fine precision.
At that tight width, it held no more than shift,
Three seams aligned, each reading true and bold.

**Chorus**
The price is in the rent you choose to pay,
Rent tight, and truth remains your only guide.
Rent wide, and world's complexities arise,
Truth veers like tides, on signs that change with time.

**Verse 2 - Wide Width**
At a wider width, where more than meets the eye,
The meter read not seam but body, not in line.
Peaks and valleys flipped as if by fate,
Wide enough to hold too much of what lies outside.

**Bridge**
So, who would pay wide when truth is scarce?
Who dares rent wide and risk a world so vast?
The composer decided on tight alone,
For tight width, the only honest one in all three forms:
Depth, address, and sign.
