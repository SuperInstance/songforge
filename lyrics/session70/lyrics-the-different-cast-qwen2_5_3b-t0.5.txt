**Verse 1 - The Crowds**
In a hall filled with echoes,  
A crowd of one voice many times,  
Repeatedly saying the same phrase,  
Thirty-two copies agreeing in their verse.

The census-taker counted twenty-four voices,  
But the ledger said thirty-two were listed,  
A deficit, the identical crowd reporting fewer members than its own roster.  

**Chorus - The Arithmetic**
In a room full of strangers,  
Four different singers speaking one line,
Thirty-two people saying it four times,  
A surplus, each new voice adding a little extra agreement.

The census-taker learns to read the roster,  
Whether the names are the same name many times or many names once. 

**Verse 2 - The Crowds**
In a hall filled with echoes again,  
A crowd of many voices diverse,  
Four strangers, four lives, four ways of saying one line.

The census-taker counted thirty-two people,  
But each new voice added a little extra agreement to the total.  

**Chorus - The Arithmetic**
In a room full of strangers,  
Thirty-two strangers reported as thirty-two point two,  
A surplus, each new voice adding a little extra agreement.

**Bridge - Learning to Count**
The census-taker learns to read the roster before counting the room:  
Whether the names are the same name many times or many names once. 

That is the whole answer — in the end,
It's not about numbers but understanding.
