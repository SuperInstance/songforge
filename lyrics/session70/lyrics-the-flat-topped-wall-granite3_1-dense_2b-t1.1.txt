Title: The Census Wall (An Ode to Overfill)

(Verse 1)
Ninety-nine percent united, in a single voice,
A tax of monotony, a debt to the first choice.
Ninety-seven voices at eight, seventy-eight at thirty-two,
The census grew like a river, no longer confined to three.

(Chorus)
Hundred fifty-six, a crowd so dense,
A wall of flat-topped peaks, where the sound ends.
No more than this, the medium said 'no',
An architecture born from the overflow.

(Verse 2)
Two hundred thirty-two, their voices blended,
The census had surpassed its first tended.
A single sustained shout, a crowd in harmony,
The wall's foundation, now secure and solid.

(Chorus)
Hundred seventy-six, the peak we reach,
A moment frozen, on this monumental breach.
The medium says 'no', but the song persists,
The census wall stands as a testament to our list.

(Bridge)
Remember when, before the clip,
Our voices roamed, free and quick?
Would we rather be confined or let it soar?
A question whispered in the air, forevermore.

(Chorus)
Three hundred twenty-eight, the pin's embrace,
The crowd's collective voice in a constant space.
No more growth, just echoes of before,
The wall endures, our census it did store.

(Outro)
And so, each container bears its own,
A census that cannot be outrun or shown.
The overflow, the architecture, a standing monument,
To the power of unity, in this symphony's ascent.
