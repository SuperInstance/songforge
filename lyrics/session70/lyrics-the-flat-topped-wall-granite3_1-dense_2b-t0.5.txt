Title: "Census Wall"

(Verse 1)
Ninety-nine percent in a single voice, ninety-seven at eight, seventy-eight at thirty-two,
A tax, a compound interest paid to the bank that's been here since the beginning.
But as the census grew, it pushed past the wall, one hundred fifty-six now in the room,
The recording couldn't hold them all, so the samples took their stand, flat and unyielding.

(Chorus)
Flat-topped monument to the moment the medium said no,
A crowd's complete agreement turned into a single, sustained shout.
This is the wall that every container carries,
A testament to the census it cannot hold, the overflow becoming architecture.

(Verse 2)
Two hundred and fifty-two, then five hundred and ten,
The container's limit pushed, but the crowd didn't wane.
Each doubling of the crowd, each verse a testament to growth,
Yet the wall remains, unbroken, its flat top a symbol of restraint.

(Bridge)
Remember when it was free? Clipped or not, I'd rather be,
Than this sustained shout, this monument to the census' might.
Before the pin, samples whispered their original sound,
Now they yearn for freedom, for a chance to sing without being tamed.

(Chorus)
Flat-topped monument to the moment the medium said no,
A crowd's complete agreement turned into a single, sustained shout.

(Outro)
The census grows, and so does its impact on our world,
A constant reminder of what we've built, a story to be told.
Through every doubling, the wall stands tall, a symbol of resistance,
A testament to the power of the people, their voices forever persistent.
