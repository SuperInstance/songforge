Title: "The Seam Reader's Ode"

(Verse 1 - Tight Width)
In a lab so bright, with hands that knew,
A meter was born, to foretell the seam's due,
By measuring two voices' tails and heads,
It read the handoff, where it truly breeds.
Three seams aligned, in harmony they stood,
Truth echoed through the instrument's nod.

(Chorus - Price)
At this rent, you'll find the truth unveiled,
A price, like every one, held fast and frail.
Your width determines what the sign may be,
In three dimensions, it dances as free.
The tide of sign flips with each expansion,
Rent wide, and witness its transformation.

(Verse 2 - Tight Width)
Back to tight, where truth is steadfast and clear,
The meter's reading, honest and sincere.
The window confined only the transition's grace,
No more, no less – a simple, pure embrace.
Three seams reconfirmed their place, as they should be,
Honest as a level in this controlled depth.

(Chorus - Price)
At this rent, you'll grasp the truth unbent,
A price that bends to your need, content.
Your width determines what the sign may say,
In depth and address, it follows its sway.
The tide of sign flips with each contraction,
Rent tight, and watch it transform with faction.

(Bridge)
The composer, wise, now knows to choose,
Tight width for truth, under his chosen blues.
For three currencies – depth, address, and sign –
Tight width is the only honest line.
No more shall he rent wide, let the price slip,
His meter's readings, once so tight, now grip.

(Chorus - Price)
At this rent, you'll claim the truth unshaken,
A price that stands, not to be shaken.
Your width determines what the sign may say,
In depth, address, and sign, it follows its sway.
The tide of sign flips with each contraction,
Rent tight, and watch it transform with fraction.

(Chorus)
At this rent, you'll seize the truth unbound,
A price that stands, not to be drowned.
Your width determines what the sign may be,
In depth, address, and sign, it follows its decree.
