(Verse 1)
In the heart of silence, where echoes once dwelled,
A musical relay begins, a tale to be told.
Four voices unite, like stars in the night,
Each with a line, a whisper, a song's first light.

(Chorus)
We carry the melody, one by one we hand,
In this dance of sound, no voice is left to stand.
The crowd, replaced by a chain, our harmony's thread,
Continuous, never fading, in this sacred spread.

(Verse 2)
First voice whispers softly, then hands it to the second,
A gentle transition, like a river's serenade.
Through the second, the melody flows, now clear and bright,
To the third, it passes, in their timbre, taking flight.

(Chorus)
We carry the rhythm, each in turn we bestow,
In this symphony of silence, no voice is left to show.
The crowd, now a chain, our unity's core,
Continuous, unbroken, in this sonic lore.

(Verse 3)
Third voice amplifies, then hands it to the fourth,
A crescendo of sound, as if nature had endorsed.
Through the fourth, the melody surges, now strong and bold,
To the first, it returns, in their depths, it unfolds.

(Chorus)
We carry the harmony, in this sacred relay,
No voice repeats, no moment of disarray.
The crowd, now a chain, our unity's core,
Continuous, eternal, in this harmonious lore.

(Bridge)
In this dance of silence, we find our collective might,
A musical relay, in the stillness of night.
No voice outshines, no moment overshadows,
Just a chain of sound, in perfect harmony grows.

(Chorus)
We carry the song, through every strand and space,
In this sacred silence, we embrace.
The crowd, now a chain, our unity's core,
Continuous, timeless, in this symphony's lore.
