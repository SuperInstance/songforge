(Verse 1)
In the heart of silence, where echoes once dwelled,
A musical relay begins its tale, a story well-told.
Four voices gather, like runners at the start,
Each with a song, about to play their part.

(Chorus)
Voices unite in harmony's embrace,
No two sing at once, no room for disgrace.
A chain of sound, no louder or loud,
Just one voice at a time, forever profound.

(Verse 2)
First whispers softly, then rise and swell,
Their song, a baton, passed with precision they tell.
With crossfades gentle, no pause or break,
This is the relay, for all to partake.

(Chorus)
Voices unite in harmony's embrace,
No two sing at once, no room for disgrace.
A chain of sound, no louder or loud,
Just one voice at a time, forever profound.

(Verse 3)
Now second voice takes up the melody,
In harmony with first, their spirits high.
Crossfade seamless, as if by magic's spell,
The crowd of silence fades, replaced by them.

(Chorus)
Voices unite in harmony's embrace,
No two sing at once, no room for disgrace.
A chain of sound, no louder or loud,
Just one voice at a time, forever profound.

(Verse 4)
Third voice enters the stage with grace,
Their song flows smoothly from the second's space.
Crossfade delicate, a rhythmic dance,
The fourth and final voice takes its chance.

(Chorus)
Voices unite in harmony's embrace,
No two sing at once, no room for disgrace.
A chain of sound, no louder or loud,
Just one voice at a time, forever profound.

(Outro)
And so the relay ends where it began,
Four voices' song, in rhythm and in span.
In this continuous loop, the crowd's place,
Now filled with harmonious voices, never to escape.
