(Verse 1)
In twilight's gentle hold, our voices blend,
Four strangers now, in quiet end.
A song, a baton, we'll not disgrace,
A relay of melody, in this place.

(Chorus)
From one to many, yet never two at once,
In harmony's chain, our song is sung.
No clamor, no volume, but a constant hum,
Each note passed gently, like a spiritum.

(Verse 2)
The crowd retreats, their roar replaced by strand,
Our voices whisper now, in this sacred land.
No echo, no reverb, just our shared sound,
A symphony of silence, profound and bound.

(Chorus)
Through hands that pass, the song doesn't cease,
In unison, it never compromises.
A dance without feet, a melody unbroken,
Each voice lending strength, no echoes woken.

(Verse 3)
The final note, from last to first appears,
As we complete this musical relay sustains our tears.
In silence's embrace, the song takes flight,
A testament to unity, in the soft moonlight.

(Chorus)
No voice louder, no notes more bold,
The song endures, its story told.
Four voices intertwined, in harmony's hold,
Our relay, a tale of unity untold.
